Data gaps

  • The S-1 copy is truncated at page F-60, so the Q1-26 interim statements are missing; Q1-26 is derived as H1 − Q2 and ties to the S-1 summary tables.
  • Quarterly cash-flow detail exists only as totals (operating, investing, financing cash flow and capex).
  • Backlog is reported only at Dec-25 and Jun-26; Starlink ARPU for Q4-25 cannot be derived from the annual figure.
  • The FY25 Starlink subscriber count comes from a table row clipped by a page break (low confidence).
