Data gaps

Four figures on this page are derived rather than reported, and the page says so wherever they appear.

  • The S-1 copy is truncated at page F-60, so the Q1-26 interim statements are missing; Q1-26 is derived as H1 minus Q2 and ties to the S-1 summary tables.
  • Quarterly cash-flow detail exists only as totals: operating, investing and financing cash flow, and capex.
  • Backlog is reported only at Dec-25 and Jun-26, and Starlink ARPU for Q4-25 cannot be derived from the annual figure.
  • The FY25 Starlink subscriber count comes from a table row clipped by a page break, so it carries low confidence.
