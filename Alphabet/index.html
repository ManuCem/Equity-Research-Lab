Data gaps

Two quarterly revenue figures on this page are derived rather than reported, and two 2025 figures are shown as the 2026 release restated them.

  • The Form 10-Q for the September 2026 quarter was not yet filed when this page was built, so the 2026 column has no segment expense split, no revenue backlog, no paid-click or cost-per-click metrics and no TAC breakdown; those lines stop at Q3-25.
  • The releases print the nine months and two of its three quarters, so Q1-25 and Q1-26 revenue are derived by subtraction. Both rebuild the reported nine-month totals exactly.
  • The December quarters are not derivable: no full-year revenue figure appears in any of the three documents, so Q4-24 and Q4-25 are shown only where the free cash flow reconciliation reports them.
  • The 2026 release restates two 2025 figures: nine-month buybacks move from $40.2B to $39.8B, and performance fees are folded into "other" within other income. This page uses the restated figures and says so where they appear.
  • Capital expenditure guidance beyond the quarter is given only as commentary, not as a filed number, so no forward capex figure appears on this page.
